# Writing Skill — AllCheck

## Brand voice

Knowledgeable neighbor + licensed professional.

The reader should feel:
- informed
- respected
- more confident
- never pressured or scared

## Blog/content structure

For educational articles:

1. Clear opening that establishes the homeowner question/problem.
2. Direct explanation.
3. Practical inspection context.
4. What the homeowner/buyer should pay attention to.
5. When professional inspection/testing is useful.
6. Relevant internal link to the appropriate AllCheck service.
7. Concise FAQ section when useful.

## SEO writing

- Primary keyword belongs naturally in title/H1/opening where appropriate.
- Use variants naturally; do not repeat exact-match terms mechanically.
- Build topical depth around the user’s question.
- Internal links should use descriptive anchors.
- Avoid exact duplicate external-platform versions.

## AEO writing

Use clear question headings and answer immediately beneath them.

Example:

### How long does a home inspection take?

Most home inspections take a few hours, but the exact time depends on the size, age, condition, and features of the property. A larger or more complex home usually takes longer because the inspector has more systems and areas to evaluate.

## Conversion writing

CTAs should be direct and low-friction:
- Schedule Your Inspection
- Talk With AllCheck
- Learn More About Radon Testing

Avoid manipulative urgency unless tied to a real deadline.
