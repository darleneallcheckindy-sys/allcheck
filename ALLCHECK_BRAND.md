# AllCheck Inspections — Brand & Business Reference

## Positioning

AllCheck Inspections is an Indianapolis-area inspection company positioned as a knowledgeable neighbor and licensed professional. The brand should make buyers, homeowners, agents, investors, and builders feel informed rather than overwhelmed.

## Core promise

Give clients clear, honest inspection information so they can move forward with confidence.

## Tagline

“Your home is inspected right when your house is AllChecked.”

## Verified / established differentiators

- 18+ years of industry experience
- Reports delivered within 24 hours
- Client participation and education
- Own inspection school / Home Inspector Institute context
- Free termite inspection for veterans when a veteran purchases a Complete Home Inspection
- Inspection binder / educational support where currently offered
- 90-day warranty where currently applicable and factually verified

## Services

- Complete Home Inspection
- Re-Inspection
- New Construction Phased Inspection
- 11-Month / End of Builder’s Warranty Inspection
- Home Maintenance Inspection
- Investor / 4-Point Inspection
- Light Commercial Inspection
- Environmental services including termite, radon, water, mold, septic and indoor air quality where offered

## Geographic focus

Primary market: Indianapolis and Central Indiana.

Known location-page targets include:
- Indianapolis
- Avon
- Carmel
- Fishers
- Greenwood
- Noblesville

Use GSC data to prioritize other locations.

## Voice

Use:
- clear
- reassuring
- educational
- professional
- specific
- homeowner-friendly

Avoid:
- alarmist language
- exaggerated claims
- vague corporate jargon
- robotic SEO copy
- overpromising

## Critical wording rules

Correct:
- “Report delivered within 24 hours.”

Incorrect:
- “Same-day report.”

Veteran offer must remain qualified:
- Free termite inspection for veterans when purchasing a Complete Home Inspection.

## Visual system

- Red: `#B0070A`
- Charcoal: `#1A1A1A`
- Gray: `#C0C0C0`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Avoid introducing blue as a primary AllCheck brand color.
